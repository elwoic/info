ELWOIC — Elamkulam Local Weather Observation And Information Centre (ഏലംകുളം പ്രാദേശിക കാലാവസ്ഥ നിരീക്ഷണ വിജ്ഞാന കേന്ദ്രം) — is an independent, student-run, non-profit community initiative focused on local weather observation, monitoring, information, awareness, and education in the Elamkulam area of Malappuram, Kerala. ELWOIC first came online on 14 September 2025 and operates independently on a non-commercial basis. ELWOIC is not a government organisation, department, or official government weather authority, and its information does not replace official warnings or advisories issued by competent authorities.

Who We Are & Identity

👤 Maintainer & Founder:

This platform is developed and maintained by Aswanth Krishna K, from Elamkulam, Kerala. First coming online on 14 September 2025, ELWOIC was created to provide reliable and accessible local weather information for the Elamkulam region and surrounding communities.

🌍 Our Mission & Nature

ELWOIC is an independent, non-commercial initiative dedicated to local weather observation, monitoring, information sharing, and educational activities focused on Elamkulam, Malappuram, Kerala. Our mission is to empower residents, farmers, and local institutions with timely and accessible micro-climatic data.

Important Information & Operational Scope

Our weather data is updated every 30 minutes using multiple sources including AccuWeather, Windy, IMD Kerala, OpenWeather, and Open-Meteo.

  • The Elamkulam Weather Station covers approximately a 15-kilometer radius around coordinates 10.892363, 76.234402 with 24/7 automated monitoring systems.
  • For technical issues with radar display, we automatically switch to Windy radar for uninterrupted monitoring.
  • No weather observation system can guarantee 100% accuracy in every circumstance. Users should exercise caution and consult official authorities for high-consequence decisions.
