1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Elamkulam Local Weather Observation & Information Centre (ELWOIC) collects, uses, stores, protects, and manages personal information submitted through its services.

This Privacy Policy applies to all official ELWOIC services, including its websites, subdomains, communication channels, registration services, volunteer programmes, weather reporting services, data request services, and any future services operated by ELWOIC unless otherwise stated.

By accessing or using ELWOIC services or voluntarily submitting personal information, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy should be read together with the ELWOIC Terms of Service and Disclaimer.

2. Information We Collect

ELWOIC respects the privacy of its users.

Simply visiting an ELWOIC website does not require visitors to provide personal information.

Personal information is collected only when a user voluntarily provides it through an official ELWOIC service.

Depending on the service being used, ELWOIC may collect information such as:

  • Full name;
  • Email address;
  • Telephone number;
  • Postal address or location where required;
  • Information contained within messages or enquiries;
  • Weather observations submitted by users;
  • Registration details;
  • Volunteer application information;
  • Data request information;
  • Feedback or suggestions;
  • Files or documents voluntarily uploaded where permitted; and
  • Any other information voluntarily provided by the user.

Users should avoid submitting information that is unnecessary for the requested service.

3. Google Sign-In

To improve security, reduce spam, prevent duplicate submissions, and assist with verifying submissions, certain ELWOIC services may require users to authenticate using a Google account.

Where Google Sign-In is used, ELWOIC generally receives only the information that the user authorizes Google to share, which may include:

  • Display name; and
  • Email address.

ELWOIC does not receive or store users' Google passwords.

Authentication is provided by Google, and users remain subject to Google's own privacy policies and terms of service when using Google Sign-In.

4. How We Use Your Information

Personal information submitted to ELWOIC is used only for legitimate operational purposes relating to ELWOIC services.

Depending on the service requested, information may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries;
  • Process registrations;
  • Review volunteer applications;
  • Verify community weather reports;
  • Process data requests;
  • Communicate regarding requested services;
  • Send weather-related communications where the user has requested or agreed to receive them;
  • Improve ELWOIC services;
  • Maintain operational records;
  • Protect the integrity and security of ELWOIC systems; and
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations where required.

ELWOIC does not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Registration with ELWOIC

Registration with ELWOIC is entirely voluntary.

Registration is not required to browse most publicly available content on ELWOIC websites.

Users who choose to register may receive access to specific ELWOIC services, which may include:

  • Weather notifications;
  • Participation in community weather activities;
  • Requested weather-related services;
  • Service updates;
  • Volunteer opportunities; or
  • Other services provided by ELWOIC.

By registering, users agree that ELWOIC may contact them regarding services they have requested or information directly related to ELWOIC.

Registration information will not be used for unrelated commercial marketing.

6. Community Weather Reports

Users may voluntarily submit weather observations through official ELWOIC reporting services.

Information submitted may be reviewed for operational purposes, including:

  • Verification of weather conditions;
  • Assessment of report accuracy;
  • Prevention of false or duplicate submissions;
  • Improvement of local weather monitoring; and
  • Publication where appropriate.

Submission of a report does not guarantee publication.

Reports may be edited, clarified, rejected, archived, or removed where necessary.

7. Artificial Intelligence (AI)

ELWOIC may use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to assist with the operation of certain services.

AI may assist with tasks including:

  • Reviewing submitted weather reports;
  • Detecting potentially inaccurate or fraudulent submissions;
  • Generating summaries;
  • Translating communications;
  • Improving clarity of submitted information; and
  • Supporting operational processes.

AI is used as an assistance tool.

Where practical, important decisions affecting users or published information are reviewed by ELWOIC before implementation.

8. Information Storage

Information submitted to ELWOIC may be securely stored using cloud-based databases, hosting providers, or other operational systems used to provide ELWOIC services.

Access to personal information is limited to authorized persons responsible for operating ELWOIC and managing the relevant service.

ELWOIC takes reasonable measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or misuse.

However, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Cookies and Technical Information

ELWOIC does not intentionally use cookies to identify individual visitors or to track users for advertising purposes.

However, the infrastructure and third-party services used to operate ELWOIC websites may automatically collect limited technical information necessary for security, performance, and network operations.

Such information may include:

  • IP address;
  • Browser type and version;
  • Operating system;
  • Device information;
  • Date and time of access;
  • Pages visited;
  • Referring website;
  • Performance logs; and
  • Security-related logs.

This technical information may be collected by service providers such as website hosting platforms, content delivery networks, security services, database providers, or authentication providers.

ELWOIC does not intentionally use this information to personally identify visitors or to create advertising profiles.

10. Third-Party Services

ELWOIC relies on trusted third-party services to operate certain features and provide weather-related information.

These services may include, but are not limited to:

  • Cloudflare;
  • Firebase;
  • Supabase;
  • Google Sign-In;
  • India Meteorological Department (IMD);
  • Open-Meteo;
  • OpenWeather;
  • Windy;
  • Email service providers; and
  • Other operational or weather-related services used by ELWOIC.

When users interact directly with third-party services, those services remain responsible for their own privacy practices.

Users are encouraged to review the privacy policies of third-party providers where applicable.

ELWOIC is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of independent third-party services.

11. Sharing of Information

ELWOIC respects the confidentiality of personal information.

Personal information submitted to ELWOIC is not sold, rented, licensed, or shared for advertising or marketing purposes.

Information may only be shared where reasonably necessary to:

  • provide a service requested by the user;
  • protect the security or integrity of ELWOIC;
  • investigate suspected misuse of ELWOIC services;
  • comply with applicable legal obligations;
  • respond to lawful requests from competent authorities; or
  • protect the rights, property, or safety of ELWOIC, its users, volunteers, or the public.

Where possible and appropriate, ELWOIC will limit the amount of personal information disclosed.

12. Data Retention

ELWOIC retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide requested services, maintain operational records, or fulfil legitimate administrative purposes.

Registration records, volunteer applications, weather reports, correspondence, and other submitted information may be retained unless the user requests deletion or applicable law requires otherwise.

Where information is no longer required, ELWOIC will make reasonable efforts to securely delete or anonymize it.

13. Requesting Deletion of Your Information

Users may request deletion of personal information they have voluntarily submitted to ELWOIC.

Requests should be made using the official contact details provided in this Privacy Policy.

Before processing a deletion request, ELWOIC may take reasonable steps to verify the identity of the requester in order to protect personal information from unauthorized deletion.

Where deletion is legally and operationally possible, ELWOIC will make reasonable efforts to permanently remove the requested personal information from its operational systems within a reasonable period.

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law, to resolve disputes, maintain operational records, or protect the integrity of ELWOIC services.

14. Children's Privacy

ELWOIC welcomes users of all ages.

Where children voluntarily submit personal information through ELWOIC services, such information will be handled in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Parents or legal guardians may contact ELWOIC to request review, correction, or deletion of a child's personal information where appropriate and legally permissible.

ELWOIC does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is voluntarily submitted through its services.

15. Security

ELWOIC takes reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Despite these efforts, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Users acknowledge that information submitted electronically is transmitted at their own discretion and risk.

16. International Users

ELWOIC is operated from the Republic of India.

Users accessing ELWOIC services from outside India do so voluntarily and acknowledge that their information will be processed and stored in accordance with applicable Indian laws and this Privacy Policy.

ELWOIC makes no representation that its services are appropriate or available for use in every jurisdiction.

Users are responsible for complying with any laws applicable in their own country when accessing ELWOIC services.

17. Changes to this Privacy Policy

ELWOIC may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect:

  • changes to ELWOIC services;
  • improvements to operational practices;
  • new technologies or service providers;
  • legal or regulatory requirements; or
  • other developments affecting the operation of ELWOIC.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published through ELWOIC's official services.

The Version Number, Effective Date, and Last Updated date shown at the beginning of this document identify the current version.

Continued use of ELWOIC services after an updated Privacy Policy becomes effective constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

22. Final Statement

ELWOIC is committed to respecting the privacy of every individual who interacts with its services.

As an independent, non-profit community weather initiative, ELWOIC seeks to collect only the information reasonably necessary to operate its services, communicate with users, and support its mission of providing localized weather information and promoting public weather awareness.

ELWOIC appreciates the trust placed in it by its users and is committed to handling personal information responsibly, transparently, and in accordance with this Privacy Policy.
