Sponsorship Acknowledgement

AirGradient

18 Feb 2026

On 18 February 2026, ELWOIC received official confirmation from AirGradient that they would sponsor one Outdoor Air Quality Monitoring device for the project at no cost.

The device was shipped internationally and successfully received via DHL on 24 February 2026.

This in-kind sponsorship (Outdoor Kit, approx. USD 125 value) supports real-time monitoring of PM2.5 and environmental parameters for the Elamkulam region.

ELWOIC sincerely thanks AirGradient for supporting this community-driven environmental monitoring initiative.

Ecowitt

23 Mar 2026

On 23 March 2026, ELWOIC received official confirmation from Ecowitt that they would support the project by providing a weather monitoring device.

The device was shipped internationally and successfully received via UPS on 20 April 2026.

This in-kind sponsorship (Wittboy Weather Station, approx. USD 200 value) supports real-time monitoring of weather parameters including temperature, humidity, rainfall, wind speed, and atmospheric pressure for the Elamkulam region.

ELWOIC sincerely thanks Ecowitt for supporting this community-driven environmental monitoring initiative.

Beetel

23 Apr 2026

On 23 April 2026, ELWOIC received official confirmation from a Beetel representative that the project would be supported with a Beetel F1 4G Fixed Wireless Phone.

The device was shipped internationally and was successfully received via courier on 05 May 2026.

This in-kind sponsorship, valued at approximately INR 3,500, supports ELWOIC in maintaining reliable, real-time communication for its community-driven environmental monitoring activities.

ELWOIC sincerely thanks Beetel for supporting this community-driven environmental monitoring initiative.

Note

ELWOIC is a non-commercial, community-supported public weather and environmental observation initiative.

Donations are voluntary and are used strictly for operational requirements such as instruments, hosting, maintenance, documentation, printing, and logistics.

Additional expenses beyond collected funds were covered personally to ensure project continuity.

All financial records, including cash donations, in-kind sponsorships, and expenses, are updated periodically for transparency.
