3. About ELWOIC

ELWOIC is an independent, non-profit, student-operated community weather observation and information initiative founded and operated by Aswanth Krishna K.

Its objectives include:

  • Promoting local weather observation;
  • Improving public access to localized weather information;
  • Supporting weather education;
  • Encouraging responsible community participation;
  • Providing weather-related services for Elamkulam and surrounding areas; and
  • Promoting scientific awareness of weather and environmental conditions.

ELWOIC operates independently and is not a Government department, statutory authority, commercial weather provider, or official meteorological agency.

ELWOIC has been acknowledged by Elamkulam Grama Panchayat, which has expressed no objection to the operation of the initiative and permitted the use of the name "Elamkulam Local Weather Observation & Information Centre."

This acknowledgement does not imply Government ownership, management, endorsement, or responsibility for ELWOIC's operations.
