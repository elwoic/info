2. About ELWOIC

Elamkulam Local Weather Observation & Information Centre (ELWOIC) is an independent, non-profit, student-operated community weather observation and information initiative founded and operated by Aswanth Krishna K.

ELWOIC was established to improve access to localized weather information, promote community participation in weather observation, encourage weather education, and support public awareness of weather conditions within Elamkulam and surrounding areas.

ELWOIC operates independently and is not established as a Government department, statutory authority, commercial weather company, or registered meteorological agency.

4. Independent Status

ELWOIC is an independent community weather initiative.

Unless specifically identified as official Government information, all observations, forecasts, analyses, weather discussions, educational material, EWA bulletins, and other publications represent the independent work of ELWOIC.

ELWOIC does not claim to be the official meteorological authority for any area.

5. Official Government Weather Information

ELWOIC maintains a registered API account issued by the India Meteorological Department (IMD) for accessing designated meteorological data and services made available through IMD's API platform.

Where available, ELWOIC may display official district-level weather warnings or other official meteorological information obtained through this authorized access.

Such information:

  • remains the responsibility and intellectual property of IMD;
  • is displayed to improve public access to official weather information; and
  • should not be interpreted as creating any partnership, endorsement, or administrative relationship between IMD and ELWOIC.

If any difference exists between information displayed by ELWOIC and information published directly by IMD, users should consider the latest information published by IMD to be the authoritative version.

6. ELWOIC Weather Information

In addition to official Government information, ELWOIC independently publishes weather-related information, which may include:

  • Local weather observations;
  • Weather forecasts;
  • Weather discussions;
  • EWA (ELWOIC Weather Alerts);
  • Weather summaries;
  • Air quality information;
  • Educational content;
  • Community weather information;
  • Weather analysis; and
  • Other weather-related services.

These publications are prepared using information available at the time of publication, including observations from ELWOIC's weather station, official meteorological information where applicable, third-party weather data, community reports, and operational analysis.

Although ELWOIC aims to provide accurate and timely information, weather conditions are inherently variable and forecasts are subject to uncertainty.

ELWOIC therefore cannot guarantee that all published information will always be complete, accurate, uninterrupted, or suitable for every purpose.

7. Local Weather Coverage

ELWOIC primarily focuses on localized weather conditions within Elamkulam and nearby areas.

Local weather conditions may differ significantly from district-wide or regional weather conditions represented in official forecasts.

ELWOIC's observations and EWA bulletins are intended to provide additional local context and should be considered alongside official Government weather information where appropriate.

8. Community Weather Reports

ELWOIC encourages responsible community participation in weather observation.

Members of the public may voluntarily submit weather-related information through official ELWOIC services, including observations such as rainfall, flooding, wind impacts, or other weather conditions where submission is permitted.

Community reports are intended to assist ELWOIC in understanding local conditions and improving situational awareness.

All submitted reports are subject to review before publication. ELWOIC may verify, edit, clarify, delay, reject, or remove any submission where considered necessary, including but not limited to circumstances involving:

  • incomplete or insufficient information;
  • suspected false or misleading reports;
  • duplicate submissions;
  • abusive or inappropriate content;
  • reports that cannot reasonably be verified; or
  • any submission that may compromise the integrity or operation of ELWOIC.

Submission of a report does not guarantee that it will be published.

9. Artificial Intelligence (AI)

ELWOIC may use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to support certain operational activities.

AI may be used for purposes including:

  • summarising weather information;
  • translating weather communications;
  • reviewing community weather reports;
  • identifying potentially inaccurate or fraudulent submissions;
  • assisting in improving the quality or clarity of submitted information; and
  • supporting operational analysis.

AI is used only as an operational assistance tool.

Where practical, significant weather information intended for publication is reviewed by ELWOIC before being released.

AI-generated outputs may occasionally contain inaccuracies and should not be interpreted as official meteorological advice.

11. Service Availability

ELWOIC is operated as a community service and is provided on a best-effort basis.

Although reasonable efforts are made to maintain reliable operation, ELWOIC services may occasionally experience interruptions due to circumstances including:

  • maintenance;
  • equipment failure;
  • weather station malfunction;
  • communication failures;
  • internet outages;
  • electrical interruptions;
  • software errors;
  • cloud service interruptions; or
  • unforeseen operational circumstances.

Where local weather station data becomes temporarily unavailable, certain information displayed by ELWOIC may be replaced, delayed, unavailable, or supplemented using other available meteorological sources where appropriate.

ELWOIC does not guarantee uninterrupted availability of any service.

12. Public Safety Notice

Weather conditions can change rapidly and may vary significantly over short distances.

Information published by ELWOIC should not be relied upon as the sole basis for decisions involving:

  • public safety;
  • emergency response;
  • evacuation;
  • disaster management;
  • aviation;
  • navigation;
  • construction safety;
  • agricultural risk management;
  • or any other activity where inaccurate weather information could result in injury, loss, or damage.

For emergencies, severe weather events, natural disasters, or situations involving public safety, users should always follow instructions issued by the appropriate Government authorities and consult official information provided by the India Meteorological Department (IMD), State Disaster Management Authorities, or other competent authorities.
