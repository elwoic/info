1. Introduction

The Elamkulam Local Weather Observation And Information Centre (ELWOIC) is an independent, student-run, non-profit community initiative established to carry out local weather observation, monitoring, information sharing, reporting, awareness, and educational activities focused on the Elamkulam area of Malappuram, Kerala, India.

2. Background & Establishment

ELWOIC first came online on 14 September 2025 and has operated independently since then. Founded and operated by Aswanth Krishna K, ELWOIC was created to bridge the gap in hyper-local meteorological data. Regional forecasts frequently miss micro-level variations in specific localities, and ELWOIC addresses this by providing reliable, community-focused observations maintained independently for public benefit.

3. Mission

To serve the Elamkulam community with timely, accurate, and clearly presented weather information to enhance local awareness, safety, agriculture, and daily preparedness.

4. Vision

To continue serving as a reliable independent community weather observation platform and expand grassroots environmental understanding across the region.

5. Core Functions & Services

Real-Time Monitoring Continuous observation of local atmospheric conditions via professional hardware.
Weather Forecasting Presentation of forecast data and outlooks relevant to our local region.
Interactive Dashboard A centralized platform for current and past weather metrics.
Historical Data Access to recorded data for reference and public analysis.
Community Reports A system for residents to share real-time local observations.
Data Request System Facility for personal or research-specific data requests.
Public Awareness Dissemination of educational insights to improve local meteorological understanding.

6. Data and Observational Scope

Our data is derived from ground-based local instruments (including Ecowitt and AirGradient hardware), radar, and satellite references, focusing on:

Temperature
Humidity
Feels Like
Wind Speed
Wind Direction (Current / Avg. 10 mins)
Wind Gust (Current)
Wind Gust (Day Max.)
Pressure (Absolute / Relative)
UVI & Solar Radiation
Rainfall & Precipitation
Air Quality Index (AQI / PM2.5)
Sky Conditions & Radar

7. Importance & Community Relevance

Kerala's dynamic weather patterns require hyperlocal insights. ELWOIC provides value for:

  • Daily planning and commuting
  • Agricultural activities and crop management
  • Students & educational institutions
  • Outdoor workers & small businesses
  • Public safety and weather awareness during sudden changes

8. Community Participation

We are a collaborative initiative. Strengthening the system involves submission of local observations, providing feedback and suggestions, and engaging with shared environmental data updates.
