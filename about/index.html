01 ABOUT ELWOIC

ELWOIC — Elamkulam Local Weather Observation And Information Centre — is an independent, student-run, non-profit initiative focused on local weather observation, monitoring, information, awareness and education.

ELWOIC first came online on 14 September 2025 and operates independently from Elamkulam on a non-commercial basis. Started by Aswanth Krishna K while studying Computer Science at higher-secondary level, the project continues to be operated and developed alongside his Diploma Mechanical Engineering studies.

Our purpose is to observe local conditions, process available weather information, study developing situations and communicate useful local weather information in an understandable form.

04 FROM OBSERVATION TO INFORMATION

More than a number
on a screen.

ELWOIC does not simply take a single weather value from an external website and display it. Local readings and outside information are considered together.

01

Collect locally

Automated systems gather current observations, while historical readings help provide context.

02

Process & compare

ELWOIC processes data, calculates derived values, examines trends and compares available sources.

03

Assess conditions

Local observations are considered alongside forecast information, radar, satellite imagery and direct observation of developing weather.

04

Share useful information

Assessments can become current conditions, short-term forecasts, nowcasts or weather alerts—and may be updated as conditions change.

SOURCES WE MAY CONSIDER

ECMWF is a numerical weather prediction source, not a satellite or radar system. ELWOIC currently examines relevant ECMWF output manually rather than retrieving numerical forecast data through a direct ELWOIC API. The availability and use of other sources varies by service and situation.

05 FORECASTS, NOWCASTS & ALERTS

What is happening.
What may happen.

Observation describes what is measured or has occurred. A forecast concerns what is expected. A nowcast focuses on what is happening now or may develop in the immediate future.

EWA REGULAR INFORMATION

ELWOIC Weather Alert

Regular weather information that may cover expected rainfall, changing weather, heat conditions, developing patterns or other locally relevant conditions.

EWAE EMERGENCY / EVENT

ELWOIC Weather Alert Emergency/Event

Shorter-notice information about an ongoing, imminent or significant weather situation that may need more immediate public attention.

The exact format and level of detail depends on the situation. ELWOIC may issue further updates when new observations or other relevant information become available. ↗More about EWA & EWAE

06 INDEPENDENT OPERATION

Local by nature.
Independent by design.

ELWOIC is independently operated by its founder and operator, Aswanth Krishna K. What began as a personal student initiative has grown through weather observation, programming, data processing, software development, analysis, forecasting and public information work.

ELWOIC is not a government organisation, government department, statutory authority, local self-government institution or government-owned service. It is not owned, operated or controlled by the Government of Kerala, Government of India, India Meteorological Department, Elamkulam Grama Panchayat or Kerala Police.

✳

About the locality

The name “Elamkulam” identifies the area ELWOIC's activities relate to; it does not indicate government ownership or official affiliation.

Panchayat relationship

The Elamkulam Grama Panchayat is aware of ELWOIC's operation and has expressed no objection. It is not the operator, owner or controlling authority of ELWOIC, and ELWOIC is not a Panchayat weather service.

!

Independent information, not an official warning. ELWOIC may consider relevant official weather information, including IMD information, but is not an official weather authority. Its assessments should not be interpreted as government warnings unless explicitly stated. For official warnings, emergency advisories and instructions, refer to IMD and competent government authorities.

07 UNDERSTANDING THE LIMITS

Weather is local.
And always changing.

Conditions can vary over short distances and periods. A reading at ELWOIC's observation site may not represent every part of Elamkulam, and a forecast is an expectation—not a guarantee.

ELWOIC assesses situations using information reasonably available at the time. New observations, model updates, changing systems, missing data or service interruptions can all change the picture. Published information may be updated, revised or replaced as new information becomes available.

Limited local observation coverageSensor characteristics and installation conditionsEquipment, internet or cloud-service availabilityMissing or delayed observationsDifferences between weather sourcesRapidly developing local conditionsNo dedicated ELWOIC radar or satellite system

Radar, satellite and numerical weather prediction information used in assessments comes from available external sources. These limitations are considered when interpreting and communicating ELWOIC information.
