01 / THE RELATIONSHIP

Official information.
Independent presentation.

The India Meteorological Department (IMD), under India's Ministry of Earth Sciences, is the country's national meteorological service. ELWOIC uses selected information made available through official IMD services as one external source in its local weather information system.

Our purpose is to make relevant official weather information available to ELWOIC's information-processing system and, where appropriate, consider it alongside local observations for Elamkulam. The information's origin remains clear: IMD information is IMD information; ELWOIC's analysis is ELWOIC's analysis.

02 / THE DATA

What ELWOIC currently uses

IMD's API reference covers several types of meteorological services. ELWOIC's current integration uses relevant district-level warning and nowcast information.

◉
IMD SERVICE 01

District-wise Nowcast

District-level nowcast warning information, with categories such as rainfall, thunderstorms, lightning probability and other weather conditions, alongside issue, validity and severity-colour information.

DEVELOPING WEATHER CONDITIONS
!
IMD SERVICE 02

District-wise Warnings

District warning information across forecast days. Documented categories include heavy rain, thunderstorms and lightning, strong surface winds, heat-wave conditions and other hazards.

REGIONAL HAZARD INFORMATION

The IMD API reference includes other categories too, such as current weather, forecasts, station-wise nowcast, subdivision-wise warnings and rainfall information. They are not all part of the current integration described here.

03 / DIFFERENT PERSPECTIVES

Why combine information?

A local observation site provides useful detail about conditions at that site, but one site cannot describe every weather system affecting a wider area. District-level information adds a broader official perspective; it does not replace local observations.

A district warning does not mean identical conditions will occur everywhere in that district. Conditions at ELWOIC's observation site also do not necessarily represent the entire district.

04 / THE CONNECTION

A controlled, server-side route

IMD's API access guidance includes public-IP whitelisting. ELWOIC therefore uses a server-side intermediary for upstream requests rather than having visitors' browsers call the IMD API directly. A Cloudflare Worker sits between ELWOIC webpages and that intermediary.

06 / INTERPRETATION

Data is not the same as an alert

Receiving an IMD warning is not the same as issuing an IMD warning. ELWOIC may consider a warning's type, affected district and validity period alongside current local observations, rainfall or cloud development, other weather information and ELWOIC's own analysis.

IMD

Official source information

The warning or nowcast information originates from the India Meteorological Department.

+
ELWOIC

Independent local context

ELWOIC may assess relevant information alongside local observations and other inputs.

i

ELWOIC does not modify an IMD warning and present the modified version as an official IMD warning. ELWOIC assessments and public information are identified as ELWOIC information and do not replace official warnings.
